Free trade negotiations between EFTA and Thailand: Delegation of the Swiss Parliament to visit Bangkok

The EFTA/EU Delegation of the Swiss Federal Assembly will travel to Thailand on a working visit from 5-9 September. The official meetings will focus on the resumption of free trade negotiations between EFTA and Thailand. The visit will take place together with parliamentary delegations from the other EFTA states Norway, Iceland and Liechtenstein.

Content

Switzerland has close economic ties with Thailand, with almost CHF 10 billion of bilateral trade volume. This makes the country Switzerland's second most important trading partner in Southeast Asia after Singapore. Conversely, Switzerland is the most important exporting country for Thailand in Europe, ahead of Germany and the Netherlands. In addition to precious metals, the main products traded are pharmaceutical products and medical technology, watches, machinery and precision instruments, as well as consumer and luxury goods.

Against this background, Switzerland is seeking a comprehensive free trade agreement with Thailand within the framework of EFTA. The negotiations concerned were resumed in June 2022. A future agreement should ensure that the Swiss export industry has the most non-discriminatory access possible to the Thai market, increase legal certainty and promote sustainable development.

The EFTA Parliamentary Committee regularly visits third countries with which EFTA is negotiating the conclusion or renewal of a free trade agreement. The talks in Bangkok are intended to provide an opportunity for the parliamentarians from the four EFTA States to obtain first-hand information on Thailand's positions in these negotiations and to reaffirm at parliamentary level the EFTA states' interest in a comprehensive, ambitious agreement.

The working visit's agenda includes meetings with Thailand's foreign minister and deputy trade minister, the two presidents of the House of Representatives and the Senate, the Thailand-Switzerland Parliamentary Friendship Group, Thailand's chief negotiator in these free trade negotiations, and business associations and civil society organisations. Also planned are virtual exchanges with the Swiss community in Thailand.

The EFTA/EU Delegation will be represented on this working visit by its President, Councillor of States Benedikt Würth (The Centre, SG), its Vice President, National Councillor Thomas Aeschi (SVP, ZG), and the two National Councillors Hans-Peter Portmann (FDP, ZH) and Nicolas Walder (The Greens, GE).
