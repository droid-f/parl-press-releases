Official visit by the President of the Council of States to London and Cambridge

Thomas Hefti, President of the Council of States, will travel to the United Kingdom with a parliamentary delegation from 21 to 24 March 2022. Their official meetings will focus on the expansion of bilateral relations between Switzerland and the United Kingdom in research and innovation.

Content

Switzerland has excellent bilateral relations with the UK, which is its fourth most important trading partner. Since the UK's withdrawal from the EU, Switzerland and the UK have negotiated various bilateral agreements that safeguard existing rights and obligations in UK-Swiss relations. "This foundation offers potential for further cooperation ‒ especially in the fields of research and innovation, trade and financial services," said Thomas Hefti (FDP, GL), President of the Council of States, ahead of his official visit.

Like Switzerland, the UK is not currently associated to the European Research Framework Programme Horizon Europe. The trip offers the opportunity to talk directly to British partners and discover what prospects the UK has for an early association and to what extent it is planning an alternative, should an association not come about.

The programme in London includes meetings with Kwasi Kwarteng, the UK Secretary of State for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy; with the science and technology committees of both the House of Lords and the House of Commons; and with Professor Christopher Smith, Executive Chair of the Arts and Humanities Research Council, part of UK Research and Innovation (UKRI). The delegation will also be received by the Speaker of the House of Lords, Lord McFall of Alcluith.

The delegation will then visit the University of Cambridge. The 813-year-old institution has produced more Nobel laureates than any other university, among them the Swiss professor Didier Queloz, with whom a discussion is scheduled. The agenda also includes meetings with Vice-Chancellor Professor Stephen J. Toope and Pro-Vice-Chancellor for Research, Professor Anne Ferguson-Smith. Furthermore, the delegation will use its stay in Cambridge to talk to Swiss researchers and students and thereby gain an understanding of the UK research landscape and of the opportunities for expanding Swiss-UK cooperation in research and innovation.

Mr Hefti will be accompanied on this trip by Council of States members Brigitte Häberli-Koller (The Centre, TG), Elisabeth Baume-Schneider (SP, JU), Mathias Zopfi (Greens, GL) and Andrea Caroni (FDP, AR) as well as by National Council member Roland Rino Büchel (SVP, SG).
