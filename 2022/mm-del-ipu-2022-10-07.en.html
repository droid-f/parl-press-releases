Press release

145th Assembly of the Inter-Parliamentary Union

The Autumn Assembly of the Inter-Parliamentary Union will take place from 11 to 15 October in Kigali (Rwanda). The theme of the conference will be Gender-sensitive parliaments and will focus on gender equality. A delegation consisting of four members of the National Council and two members of the Council of States will represent the Swiss Parliament at the conference.

Content

At the invitation of the Rwandan Parliament, the 145th Assembly of the Inter-Parliamentary Union (IPU) will take place in Kigali (Rwanda) from 11 to 15 October. Initially scheduled for October 2020, the assembly has been postponed twice owing to the global health situation. More than 1,000 parliamentarians are expected to attend.

At the assembly, the situation in Ukraine will once again be on the agenda. The IPU Task Force for the peaceful resolution of the war in Ukraine will present a report of its visit to Moscow and Kyiv. Delegations from Ukraine and Russia are also expected to attend.

The Standing Committee on Democracy and Human Rights will adopt a resolution aimed at combating human trafficking and finding ways to address human rights’ violations, including those sponsored by the state. A special section of the assembly’s general debate will be devoted to the topic of follow-up action by its members to IPU resolutions – an important subject for the delegation.

The Swiss delegation will be made up of the following parliamentarians:

  1. Daniel Jositsch (SP/ZH), Council of States member, Delegation President
  2. Thomas Hurter (SVP/SH), National Council member, Delegation Vice-President
  3. Christine Badertscher (The Greens/BE), National Council member
  4. Andrea Caroni (FDP/AR), Council of States member
  5. Laurence Fehlmann Rielle (SP/GE), National Council member
  6. Laurent Wehrli (FDP/VD), National Council member

The delegation will take advantage of the assembly to meet with other national delegations and to learn about the work of the regional office of the Swiss Agency for Development and Cooperation (SDC) in Kigali. The delegation will be shown projects supported by the SDC, particularly in connection with dual-track vocational education and training.
