Council presidents to visit the European Parliament

On Wednesday, 5 October, National Council President Irène Kälin (Greens, AG) and Council of States President Thomas Hefti (FDP, GL) will pay a working visit to the European Parliament in Strasbourg. They will be accompanied by First Vice- President of the National Council Martin Candinas (The Centre, GR) and First Vice- President of the Council of States Brigitte Häberli-Koller (The Centre, TG). On the agenda are meetings with European Parliament President Roberta Metsola (EPP, Malta), European Commission Vice-President Maroš Šefčovič and MEPs from various parliamentary groups.

Content

The discussions will focus on the ongoing exploratory talks between Switzerland and the EU on a possible new package approach. The talks with European Parliament President Roberta Metsola and European Commission Vice President Maroš Šefčovič are intended to provide an opportunity to learn first-hand about the EU's position in these talks and the prospect of starting negotiations. The main issues to be discussed cover possible institutional solutions with regard to dynamic alignment and dispute settlement, the contentious issues in the area of the free movement of persons, and the planned extension of negotiation topics to possible new agreements in the areas of electricity, health and food safety agreements.

The discussion with President Metsola will also include the war in Ukraine, the sanctions policy against Russia, and the possible ways of increasing cooperation between Switzerland and the EU in the area of security and defence and in overcoming the energy crisis.

In an exchange with the chair of the Committee on Industry, Research and Energy, MEP Cristian-Silviu Bușoi (EPP, Romania), and a further committee member, MEP Christian Ehler (EPP, Germany), the Council presidents will underline the importance of Switzerland's rapid association to the EU's Horizon Europe research programme. Switzerland's present exclusion from this programme is negatively affecting existing research networks and the European research landscape as a whole.

A meeting with the chair of the Committee on the Internal Market and Consumer Protection, MEP Anna Cavazzini (Greens/EFA, Germany), is also planned. In addition to bilateral issues, this meeting will focus on the impact of the Ukraine war on the EU's internal market.

The Swiss delegation will exchange views on the state of bilateral relations, the planned European Parliament report on Swiss-EU relations, and the importance of interparliamentary dialogue with the chair of the delegation responsible for relations with Switzerland, MEP Andreas Schwab (EPP, Germany), Rapporteur of the Committee on Foreign Affairs for relations with Switzerland, Lukas Mandl (EPP, Austria), as well as shadow rapporteurs Andreas Schieder (S&D, Austria) and Christophe Grudler (Renew, France).
