Meeting of the OECD global parliamentary network in Riga

Council of States member Ruedi Noser (FDP, ZH) and National Council member Prisca Birrer-Heimo (SP, LU) will be representing the Standing Parliamentary Delegation for Participation in OECD Activities at a global parliamentary network meeting of the Organisation for Economic Cooperation and Development (OECD) in Riga, Latvia.

Content

The OECD’s global parliamentary network provides parliamentarians from member states with a platform to exchange views on policy issues relating to the economy, finance, education, science, society, the environment and development.

On Thursday, 30 June, and Friday, 1 July, a conference on ‘Building a secure and inclusive digital future in a post-COVID world’ will be held in Riga at the invitation of the Latvian Parliament. Members of Parliament from around 30 countries will discuss the challenges of digital transformation in democratic processes, and exchange views with experts from the OECD on issues of governance in the digital age, cyber security and combating digital mis- and disinformation.

The PD-OECD will be represented at the event by its president, Councillor of States Ruedi Noser (FDP, ZH) and its vice-president, National Councillor Prisca Birrer-Heimo (SP, LU).
