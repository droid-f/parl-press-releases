APK-S delegation on a working visit to Berlin

A six-member delegation representing the Council of States Foreign Affairs Committee (FAC-S) will visit Germany from 21 to 23 November. The delegation’s meetings will concentrate primarily on Swiss–German bilateral relations, with a particular focus on the impact of the war in Ukraine and challenges in European policy.

Content

Switzerland and Germany maintain diverse and close ties, linked by a common language and active dialogue on economic, cultural and social issues. Following their fact-finding trip to the United States last year, the Committee decided to turn its attention to Germany, one of its neighbouring countries and Switzerland’s largest trading partner.

During their working visit to Berlin the delegation will meet with representatives of their parliamentary counterparts, the Bundesrat Committee on Foreign Affairs. They will also hold talks with representatives of the German parliament, including members of the Foreign Affairs Committee, the European Affairs Committee and the parliamentary group on Swiss–German relations. Their meetings will focus on the countries’ bilateral relations, while also addressing the impact that current challenges in Swiss–EU relations are having on cooperation between Switzerland and Germany in business and research. Other topics will include how to respond to the Russian war of aggression in Ukraine and its consequences for Germany and Switzerland.

In addition to those at parliamentary level, there will be meetings with representatives of the Federal Foreign Office, the German Chancellery and the Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Climate Action. Here the delegation will learn more about central elements of current German foreign policy, with a particular focus on Germany’s experience holding the G7 presidency and the challenges involved with the energy transition and energy supply security. The FAC-S delegation will also visit Stadler Rail Deutschland and discuss business, research and culture with experts.

The Foreign Affairs Committees send delegations abroad annually. This year’s FAC-S delegation is headed by committee president Pirmin Bischof (The Centre/SO) and comprised of Council of States members Andrea Gmür-Schönenberger (The Centre/LU), Andrea Caroni (FDP/AR), Daniel Jositsch (SP/ZH), Matthias Michel (FDP/ZG) and Thomas Minder (parteilos/SH).
