Switzerland’s Association to Horizon Europe: Delegation from the Committee for Science, Education and Culture to visit Brussels

Six members of the National Council’s Committee for Science, Education and Culture (SECC) will pay a working visit to Brussels on 10 and 11 October. The delegation will meet with key stakeholders from the field of research in Europe, Members of the European Parliament, representatives of the European Commission and the British Ambassador to the European Union. The talks will focus on Switzerland’s association to the EU research programme Horizon Europe, which is blocked at present.

Content

Since June 2021, Switzerland has been considered a non-associated third country to Horizon Europe and to programmes and initiatives related to it. This means that Swiss researchers can only participate in calls for proposals to a limited extent and cannot assume project management. This non-association status diminishes Switzerland’s appeal as a centre for research and innovation.

The SECC, as the competent parliamentary committee, is closely following the consequences of this non-association status and the efforts to start negotiations. It notes with concern that the talks between the Federal Council and the European Commission are still blocked. The SECC has affirmed many times that negotiations should aim to achieve full association. This concern is becoming more urgent as major calls for proposals as part of Horizon Europe are due in 2023. In view of the ongoing exploratory talks between Switzerland and the EU, the delegation’s visit to Brussels aims to encourage the opening of negotiations soon.

On the delegation’s agenda is a meeting with MEPs from the European Parliament’s Committee on Industry, Research and Energy and from the Committee on Foreign Affairs. The delegation will also meet with Deputy Secretary-General of the European Commission John Watson and with British Ambassador to the EU Lindsay Croisdale-Appleby, as well as with representatives of European research associations.

The SECC will be represented on the visit by its chair, Fabien Fivaz (The Greens, NE), and National Council members Diana Gutjahr (SVP, TG), Valérie Piller Carrard (SP, FR), Thomas Brunner (The Green Liberals, SG), Christian Wasserfallen (FDP, BE) and Philipp Kutter (The Centre, ZH).
