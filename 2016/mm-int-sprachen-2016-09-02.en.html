Christa Markwalder, President of the National Council, on official working visit to Georgia, Ukraine and Moldova

From 5 to 10 September the President of the National Council, Christa Markwalder (FDP/BE), will be visiting Georgia, Ukraine and Moldova at the invitation of her counterparts in the three countries. Official talks will focus on bilateral relations and the situation in the local regions.

Content

​During a two-day visit to the Georgian capital Tbilisi, the President of the National Council, Christa Markwalder, will meet for talks with the parliamentary speaker David Usupashvili, President Giorgi Margvelashvili and the foreign minister Mikheil Janelidze. Meetings are also planned with other members of the Georgian parliament and of the Georgian-Swiss parliamentary friendship group. Issues under discussion will be the free-trade agreement between the European Free Trade Association (EFTA) and Georgia signed on the occasion of the EFTA ministerial meeting in Bern on 27 June 2016.

Ms Markwalder’s visit to the Ukrainian capital Kiev will begin in the evening of 6 September with a showing of the film ‘Mais im Bundeshaus’, followed by a panel discussion with members of the Ukrainian parliament. On the following two days, talks will be held with the parliamentary speaker Andry Paruby, President Petro Poroshenko, Prime Minister Volodymyr Hroysman and the foreign minister, Pavlo Klimkin. Meetings with other members of parliament and Swiss companies operating in Ukraine are also planned.

On 9 September Ms Markwalder will be in Chişinău, the capital of Moldova, to take part in a ceremony to mark the 25th anniversary of the country’s independence. Bilateral talks are also planned with the parliamentary speaker Andrian Candu, Prime Minister Pavel Filip and the foreign minister Andrei Galbur, and with further members of the Moldovan parliament.
