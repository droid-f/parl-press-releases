Press release

President of the National Council pays official working visit to Croatia and Hungary

At the invitation of her Croatian and Hungarian counterparts, the President of the National Council, Christa Markwalder, (FDP. The Liberals/BE) will visit Zagreb on 11 and 12 April before moving on to Budapest between 13 and 15 April. Discussions will cover bilateral relations, the implementation of Switzerland’s enlargement con-tribution for new EU member states, economic development in Eastern and South-eastern Europe and the refugee crisis as a challenge for Europe.

Content

​At the invitation of her Croatian counterpart, National Council president Christa Markwalder will visit Zagreb on 11 and 12 April. Her two-day stay will include an official meeting and working lunch with the president of Croatia’s parliament, Željko Reiner. She will also pay courtesy visits to Croatia’s president, Kolinda Grabar-Kitarović, the prime minister, Tihomir Orešković, and the foreign minister, Miro Kovač. The working visit to Croatia will be rounded off with a meeting with the mayor of Samobor and a tour of the production plant of a Swiss company which is a market leader in the production of electrical insulation paper.

In the second half of the week National Council president Christa Markwalder will be taking up the invitation of her Hungarian counterpart to hold official talks in Budapest between 13 and 15 April. During her stay there will be an official meeting and working lunch with the president of the Hungarian parliament, László Kövér. She will also pay courtesy visits to Hungary’s president, János Áder, the economics minister, Mihály Varga, and hold a working meeting with members of the parliamentary Hungary-Switzerland friendship group. In addi-tion, Christa Markwalder will be discussing projects related to the implementation of Switzer-land’s enlargement contribution for new EU member states and is planning to visit a Swiss company, which is a leading producer of inductive and photoelectric sensors.

On Wednesday, 14 April, the National Council president will take up an invitation on the part of the Centre for Democracy Research at Andrássy University Budapest and the Swiss em-bassy and give a talk on the subject of Switzerland and Europe. The event will take place at 6pm in the university’s hall of mirrors and is open to the public.

The President of the National Council will be accompanied by a parliamentary delegation including National Council member and first vice president Jürg Stahl (SVP/ZH) and National Council members Viola Amherd (CVP/VS), Balthasar Glättli (GP/ZH), Tiana Angelina Moser (GLP/ZH), Philippe Nantermod (FDP. The Liberals/VS) and Roger Nordmann (SP/VD). Also joining the delegation are Philippe Schwab, secretary general of the Federal Assembly and Cédric Stucky, foreign policy advisor.
