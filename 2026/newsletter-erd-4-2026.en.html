Fourth part-session 2026

Content

The fourth part-session of the Parliamentary Assembly of the Council of Europe took place in Strasbourg from 28 September to 2 October 2026. As the autumn session of the Federal Assembly was taking place in Bern at the same time, the Swiss delegation was only able to attend the first day of the part-session. National Councillors Roland Rino Büchel (SVP, SG), Damien Cottier (FDP, NE) and Fabian Molina (SP, ZH) travelled to Strasbourg for this purpose.

For this reason, no separate session review by the Swiss delegation is being published for this part-session. An overview of the topics discussed and the decisions taken by the Assembly is available on the website of the Parliamentary Assembly of the Council of Europe.
