Visits made abroad

Official visit by the president of the National Council to Qatar

On the return journey from Nepal, the president of the National Council, Pierre-André Page (SVP, FR), paid an official visit to Qatar on 8 and 9 November, at the invitation of the president of the Shura Council, Hassan bin Abdulla Al-Ghanim.

Content

In Doha, discussions between Mr Page and his Qatari counterpart focused on bilateral relations between Switzerland and Qatar, as well as on prospects for strengthening cooperation between the two countries, particularly in the areas of good offices and mediation. Mr Page highlighted the shared tradition between Switzerland and Qatar in this area and commended Qatar’s active role in mediation efforts and diplomatic initiatives, particularly in relation to the situation in Gaza and relations between Iran and the United States. This cooperation is based, in particular, on two letters of intent signed in 2025 between Switzerland and Qatar, which relate respectively to mediation and facilitation, and to humanitarian aid and development. Both sides reaffirmed their commitment to using their good offices to promote dialogue and the search for peaceful solutions to conflicts. Mr Page also emphasised the complementary role of parliamentary diplomacy, which helps to foster dialogue and support the peaceful resolution of conflicts.

Against a backdrop of high regional tensions, the discussions also focused on maritime security issues, particularly in the Strait of Hormuz and the Strait of Bab el-Mandeb, as well as their implications for international trade and shipping. The two sides emphasised the importance of safeguarding freedom of navigation and expressed the hope that a solution could be found to ensure the resumption of maritime traffic under safe conditions.

Beyond security issues, the visit by the National Council president also provided an opportunity to discuss the prospects for developing economic relations between the two states. Opportunities to strengthen trade and investment were highlighted, especially in the sustainable waste management sector, which offers prospects for cooperation based on Swiss expertise and know-how.

Following on from these discussions, Mr Page also met with members of the Swiss Business Council to discuss the specific characteristics of the Qatari market, market access conditions and the opportunities it offers Swiss companies, as well as the impact of the regional security situation on their operations.

A further topic covered during the visit was vocational education and training. A themed lunch focusing on Switzerland’s dual-track system brought together the Under-Secretary of the Qatari Ministry of Education and Higher Education, Ibrahim Bin Saleh Al-Naimi, representatives of Swiss pharmaceutical companies interested in introducing this model in Qatar, and officials from Qatari universities and higher education institutions. The meeting provided an opportunity to outline the distinctive features of the Swiss education system and to explore opportunities for cooperation in this area.

Finally, the Mr Page joined the Swiss delegation, comprising representatives from the Federal Department of the Environment, Transport, Energy and Communications and the Federal Department of Foreign Affairs, for the opening ceremony of the Plenipotentiary Conference of the International Telecommunication Union (ITU PP-26), which is being held in Doha from 9 to 27 November. The conference, which is the ITU’s supreme decision-making body, brings together member states every four years to set the organisation’s strategic direction and agree on the future of international cooperation in the fields of telecommunications and digital technology.

The National Council president was accompanied by National Council members Roland Rino Büchel (SVP, SG) and Giorgio Fonio (The Centre, TI).
