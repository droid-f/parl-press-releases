President of the National Council and the Office of the National Council to pay visit to Canada

The president of the National Council, Pierre-André Page (SVP, FR), will be in Canada from 12 to 17 April with a parliamentary delegation comprising the vice presidents of the Council and parliamentary group leaders. On the agenda are high-level meetings with the Speaker of the House of Commons, Francis Scarpaleggia, and the Speaker of the Senate, Raymonde Gagné, as well as a courtesy visit with Foreign Minister Anita Anand.

Content

The primary aim of the visit is to strengthen bilateral relations and gain a better understanding of how Canada views the development of its economic and trade relations with Switzerland, as well as its position within the current global geopolitical landscape, particularly in North America. Canada is Switzerland’s second-largest trading partner on the (North) American continent, in terms of both bilateral trade and direct investment. The two countries also share many similarities, particularly in their commitment to democratic values, multilingualism and federalism.

After stopping in Ottawa, the National Council president and his delegation will travel to Montreal and then to Quebec City from 15 to 17 April. This visit to the French-speaking part of Canada will provide an opportunity for dialogue with the Swiss community and local business circles. A meeting is also scheduled with the Speaker of the National Assembly of Quebec, Nathalie Roy, and other members of the Assembly. The programme will be rounded off with courtesy visits to representatives of Indigenous peoples, as well as to the Lieutenant Governor of Quebec, Manon Jeannotte.

The delegation, led by National Council president Pierre-André Page (SVP, FR), comprises the first vice president, Katja Christ (GLP, BS), the second vice president, Farah Rumy (SP, SO), parliamentary group leaders Thomas Aeschi (SVP, ZG), Yvonne Bürgin (The Centre, ZH), Damien Cottier (FDP, NE) and Corina Gredig (GLP, ZH), parliamentary group vice president Greta Gysin (The Greens, TI) and parliamentary group co-president Samira Marti (SP, BL).
