Visit to Mexico by a delegation from the Council of States’ Foreign Affairs Committee

A delegation from the Council of States Foreign Affairs Committee (FAC-S), led by its chair, Council of States member Carlo Sommaruga, will pay a visit to Mexico from 22 to 27 November. This official visit aims to strengthen parliamentary relations between Switzerland and Mexico, while addressing the modernisation of the free trade agreement and key issues relating to human rights and regional development.

Content

Once a year, it is customary for a delegation from the FAC-S to undertake a fact-finding trip abroad. This year, a delegation comprising four members of the FAC-S will travel to Mexico.

Busy schedule in Mexico City: politics and economics

The visit will begin in Mexico City, where the delegation will meet with Mexico’s most senior officials. The discussions will focus, in particular, on the current political situation and an assessment of the first few years of President Claudia Sheinbaum’s government. The delegation will visit the Secretariat of Foreign Affairs and the Senate of the Republic to discuss foreign policy and economic issues.

Economic issues will also be a key focus, with meetings planned with the Swiss-Mexican Chamber of Commerce (Swisscham) and a visit to the Secretariat of Economy to discuss bilateral trade relations and current and future challenges relating to the free trade agreement that came into force in 2001. The delegation will also visit the Novartis facilities and the Colegio Suizo, highlighting the vitality of Switzerland’s presence in the country.

Focus on development and human rights in Oaxaca

The second part of the trip will take place in Oaxaca. The delegation will hold talks with the state governor on issues relating to regional economic development. Particular emphasis will be placed on societal and security issues: A meeting with civil society representatives and experts will provide an opportunity to address sensitive issues such as human rights, disappearances and the rights of indigenous peoples.

Finally, the delegation will visit the start-up COAA, which specialises in the recycling of mezcal waste. Stemming from a technical exchange with ETH Zurich on sustainable construction technologies, this partnership has led to the development of the ‘Grounded Materials Mexico’ programme, with the support of the Mexican Ministry of Culture.

The trip will enable the Council of States Foreign Affairs Committee to gain first-hand insight into the opportunities for cooperation and the shared challenges facing Switzerland and Mexico.

Led by the committee chair, Council of States member Carlo Sommaruga (SP, GE), this year’s FAC-S delegation comprises Council members Marco Chiesa (SVP, TI), Daniel Jositsch (Independent, ZH) and Beat Rieder (The Centre, VS).
