President of the National Council to visit the Central African Republic and Cameroon

Ahead of the 51st annual session of the French-speaking Parliamentary Assembly (Assemblée parlementaire de la Francophonie, APF), to be held in Yaoundé from 9 to 12 July, the President of the National Council, Pierre-André Page (SVP, FR), will pay an official visit to the Central African Republic and Cameroon.

Content

The President of the National Council, Pierre-André Page, will travel to the Central African Republic on 6 July. The visit will focus on Switzerland’s international cooperation, which has run a humanitarian office in Bangui since 2022. The programme includes official talks with parliamentary and government representatives, as well as visits to a number of international and Swiss partner organisations that support the local civilian population.

Switzerland has long been actively engaged in humanitarian aid and transitional justice in the Central African Republic. The visit will give Mr Page a first-hand look at political and security developments in the country, as well as Switzerland’s role there.

Mr Page will then continue on to Yaoundé for a series of visits on 8 July. This includes official talks at the National Assembly and meetings with members of the Switzerland–Cameroon parliamentary friendship group, as well as a visit to the Institut Agricole d'Obala, a training centre for agriculture and agrotechnology that works closely with Swiss institutions. He will also meet representatives of the Swiss community in Cameroon and the private sector.

Switzerland and Cameroon enjoy good relations, particularly in the areas of the economy, trade and migration. Through this visit, Mr Page aims to further strengthen parliamentary ties between the two countries. Following the bilateral meetings, he will take part in the 51st session of the APF in Yaoundé.
