Official visit by the president of the National Council to Nepal

The president of the National Council, Pierre-André Page, paid a visit to Nepal from 2 to 7 November, at the invitation of the speaker of the House of Representatives of the Nepalese Parliament, Dol Prasad Aryal. Organised to mark the 70th anniversary of diplomatic relations between Switzerland and Nepal, the visit served to deepen political and parliamentary dialogue between the two countries and to highlight several decades of cooperation.

Content

In Kathmandu, Mr Page held talks with his counterpart, Dol Prasad Aryal, and with Narayan Prasad Dahal, chairperson of the National Assembly. He met with members of the Nepal–Switzerland Parliamentary Friendship Group, as well as several chairs and members of parliamentary committees, and was then received by President Ram Chandra Paudel. Discussions centred on bilateral relations, the functioning of institutions in the context of Nepal’s recent political transition, and the prospects for cooperation between the two countries.

One of the main topics of discussion was federalism. Since the adoption of its Constitution in 2015, Nepal has been continuing to implement its federal system. The discussions covered the functioning of the Swiss federal system and the challenges associated with implementing federalism in Nepal, particularly the distribution of powers and the functioning of institutions.

Peacebuilding and transitional justice – areas in which Switzerland has been supporting Nepal for several years – were also discussed. Against the backdrop of the recent protest movements led by Generation Z and the political changes that followed, Pierre-André Page also held discussions with representatives of the new authorities and the youth movement regarding their expectations and the country’s political outlook.

The situation in the regions affected by flooding on 26 August also featured prominently on the agenda. In Bidur, in the Nuwakot district, Mr Page met with the mayor and those affected by the disaster to assess the extent of the damage and the reconstruction needs. Accompanied by experts from the International Centre for Integrated Mountain Development (ICIMOD), the delegation discussed the effects of climate change in the Himalayas and the challenges relating to resilience to natural hazards. Switzerland has allocated USD 3 million towards emergency humanitarian aid.

Vocational education and training was another theme of the visit. The delegation visited Laxmi Intercontinental Auto Services Pvt. Ltd., a company which, with the support of Swiss cooperation, is helping to develop dual-track vocational education and training in Nepal. Discussions with the apprentices highlighted the potential of this model to meet the demand for skilled labour and to offer young people prospects for entering the workforce.

The visit also highlighted the results of several decades of Swiss cooperation in Nepal. Since the 1960s, this cooperation has, among other things, enabled the construction of some 10,000 suspension bridges, making it easier for rural communities to access schools, markets and essential services. It has also helped to support the development of the carpet-making industry, providing Tibetan refugees with opportunities to earn an income whilst helping to preserve a craft tradition that is now recognised internationally.

The celebrations marking the 70th anniversary of diplomatic relations were highlighted by the opening of Nhu Pukhu Park in Lalitpur, attended by the National Council president and the city’s mayor. The delegation also attended the opening of the photography exhibition ‘Halfway between earth and sky:’ In the footsteps of Ella, a documentary about the Swiss explorer Ella Maillart’s journey through Nepal.

The visit served to reaffirm the strength of relations between Switzerland and Nepal, as well as the shared commitment to continuing and deepening cooperation between the two countries, particularly in the parliamentary, institutional and economic spheres, and in tackling climate and environmental challenges.

Mr Page was accompanied on the visit by National Council members Roland Rino Büchel (SVP, SG), Giorgio Fonio (The Centre, TI) and Corina Gredig (Green Liberal Party, ZH).
