IPU parliamentary hearing at UN in New York

On 17 and 18 February, members of the Swiss IPU delegation will attend the annual parliamentary hearing at the UN in New York, which will address the issue of ‘Education as a key to peace and sustainable development’. The Swiss delegation in New York will be represented by its president Christian Lohr (National Council, CVP/TG), its vice president Daniel Jositsch (Council of States, SP/ZH) and Laurent Wehrli (National Council, FDP/VD).

Content

​On 17 and 18 February, parliamentarians from all the member states of the Interparliamentary Union (IPU) will meet at the UN headquarters in New York for the annual parliamentary hearing. Discussions will focus on Sustainable Development Goal 4 in Agenda 2030: Ensure inclusive and equitable quality education and promote lifelong learning opportunities for all.

At this year’s joint hearing, the IPU and the UN will call for greater political determination to fully implement this sustainable development goal on education by 2030. UN Deputy Secretary-General Amina J. Mohammed will launch the two-day hearing with a keynote speech entitled ‘Realising the SDGs is the only way forward’.

The IPU delegation will also visit Switzerland’s permanent representation to the UN and the Swiss General Consulate in New York in order to learn about Switzerland’s work in the UN and its presence in the city. The Swiss IPU delegation will be represented on this trip by its president Christian Lohr (National Council, CVP/TG), its vice president Daniel Jositsch (Council of States, SP/ZH) and Laurent Wehrli (National Council, FDP/VD), who is a member of the IPU’s Office of the Committee on UN Affairs.
