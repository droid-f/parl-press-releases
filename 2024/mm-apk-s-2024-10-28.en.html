FAC-S delegation to visit Denmark and Norway

A delegation from the Council of States Foreign Affairs Committee (FAC-S), led by Committee president and Council of States member Marco Chiesa, will visit Denmark and Norway from 28 October to 1 November. This is the annual FAC-S fact-finding trip, which is taking place in Europe this year. The trip is intended to foster the already good relations between Switzerland and Denmark and Norway. The delegation is set to discuss migration matters and European policy. The programme includes meetings with representatives from politics, business and civil society, and a visit to an asylum centre.

Content

Denmark​

Relations between Switzerland and Denmark are excellent. Denmark is a like-minded state and partner, particularly in the areas of democracy, international law and development cooperation. Economically, Denmark is Switzerland's second most important export destination among the Nordic countries. In addition to the important aspect of cultivating bilateral relations, the FAC-S delegation is seeking to gain insights into Denmark's migration and immigration policy on the ground and exchange views with politicians, government representatives and civil society actors.

Norway

Bilateral relations between Switzerland and Norway are also excellent. Close and effective cooperation has developed, particularly within the framework of EFTA, partly as a result of the two countries’ different relationships with the EU. Like Switzerland, Norway is not a member of the EU. However, in contrast to Switzerland, Norway is a member of the EEA. The FAC-S delegation’s visit to Norway will focus on European policy issues, with a particular emphasis on Norway's experiences within the EEA.

Since the outbreak of the war in Ukraine, Norway has become Europe's most important supplier of oil and gas. Against this backdrop, the FAC-S delegation will take a closer look at Norway's energy and security policy and its significance for Europe.

The Foreign Affairs Committees send a delegation on an annual fact-finding trip abroad. This year the delegation is being led by Committee president and Council of States member Marco Chiesa (SVP, TI), accompanied by Committee members Matthias Michel (FDP, ZG), Tiana Moser (GLP, ZH) and Beat Rieder (The Centre, VS).
