Plenary meeting of the OECD Global Parliamentary Network in Paris

The Standing Parliamentary Delegation for Participation in OECD Activities will take part in the plenary meeting of the OECD Global Parliamentary Network in Paris from 3-4 April 2024. The delegation will be represented by its president, National Council member Sophie Michaud Gigon (The Greens, VD), its vice president, Council of States member Erich Ettlin (The Centre, OW), National Council members Michael Graber (SVP, VS) and Paolo Pamini (SVP, TI), and Council of States member Eva Herzog (SP, BS). In addition to the plenary meeting, the delegation will hold bilateral talks with key OECD contacts, including Secretary-General Mathias Cormann.

Content

The plenary meeting of the OECD Global Parliamentary Network will take place from 3-4 April 2024 in Paris. This annual meeting provides an opportunity for parliamentarians to discuss with OECD experts current issues in the OECD's areas of expertise. The event is also an ideal platform for parliamentary diplomacy.

A wide range of topics will be covered: the OECD will review the global economic outlook, take stock of the energy markets, report on the latest PISA survey and discuss its trade strategy. Parliamentarians will also discuss the governance of artificial intelligence, the climate crisis and ways to strengthen democratic values and processes that are under threat in times of multiple crises.

In addition, the Swiss delegation will meet with experts from the OECD's Economics Department to discuss the main recommendations of the OECD's Economic Survey of Switzerland, which was published on 14 March.

The delegation will also hold a number of bilateral talks on the sidelines of the meeting. In particular, the delegation will meet Secretary-General Mathias Cormann and the Executive Director of the International Energy Agency, Fatih Birol. Meetings are also planned to discuss the work of the Inclusive Forum on Carbon Mitigation Approaches (IFCMA), recent developments in the area of taxes and the fight against corruption.​
