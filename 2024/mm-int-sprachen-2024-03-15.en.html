Friday, 15 March 2024 12h00

Visits made abroad

National Council President Eric Nussbaumer and Council of States President Eva Herzog travel to the USA for a working visit

National Council President Eric Nussbaumer (SP, BL) and Council of States President Eva Herzog (SP, BS) will be visiting New York and Washington DC from 18 to 22 March, accompanied by a parliamentary delegation. This working visit will focus on Switzerland's work in the UN Security Council and relations between Switzerland and the United States of America.

Content

The USA is Switzerland's second-largest trading partner worldwide, the most important destination for Swiss exports and direct investments abroad and a key hub for education and research. On 1 January 2023, Switzerland took up a place on the UN Security Council for the first time. As a non-permanent member of the Council, which is based in New York, its two-year term will run until 31 December 2024.

Mr Nussbaumer and Ms Herzog’s delegation will first travel to New York, where they will focus on Switzerland's work in the United Nations in order to gain an insight into the processes of the UN Security Council and the activities of the UN General Assembly. The delegation will also examine Switzerland's role in UN organisations, decision-making processes and current dynamics. The programme in New York also includes meetings with business representatives and a visit to the Swiss Institute, which is an important platform for contemporary Swiss art in the USA.

On 20 March, the delegation will travel to Washington DC, where talks are planned with representatives of the US Congress, the US Department of State and the US Chamber of Commerce. In addition to bilateral relations, the focus will be on US foreign economic policy, global challenges and cooperation in the area of research and innovation.

Eric Nussbaumer, National Council president and Eva Herzog, Council of States president will be accompanied on this visit by Maja Riniker (FDP, AG), first vice president of the National Council; Andrea Caroni (FDP, AR), first vice president of the Council of States; Greta Gysin (The Greens, TI), National Council member; and Werner Salzmann (SVP, BE), Council of States member.
