National Council president Dominique de Buman pays official visit to Senegal and Guinea

National Council president Dominique de Buman (CVP/FR) will travel to Senegal and Guinea between 13 and 16 October at the invitation of his respective counterparts. Official talks are expected to cover bilateral relations, as well as regional and global issues.

​
During his stay in the Senegalese capital, Dakar, Mr de Buman will hold a talk with his counterpart Moustapha Niasse. Meetings are also planned with representatives from the commodities sectors. On the evening of 13 October, Mr de Buman will deliver a speech as guest of honour at the inauguration of the renovated Swiss embassy in Dakar.

Switzerland recognised Senegal’s independence in 1960, and the two states established diplomatic relations the following year. Switzerland and Senegal foster close ties across a wide range of fields. Regional security and stability and the intensification of trade exchanges form the basis for relations between the two countries.

Mr de Buman will be received for talks by the president of the National Assembly, Claude Kory Kondiano, in the Guinean capital, Conakry, on 15 October. Discussions on health and a visit to the hospital ship Africa Mercy, currently docked in the port of Conakry, are also planned. At a gathering of representatives from Swiss firms, the discussion is likely to focus on the economic outlook in West Africa. That will be followed by a visit to the local office of the International Organization for Migration (IOM) where topical issues will be addressed.

Switzerland recognised Guinea’s independence in 1958 and diplomatic relations were established in 1960. The two countries have since signed a series of bilateral agreements, primarily on trade, investment protection, civil international aviation, technical cooperation and migration.

Mr de Buman will be accompanied on his trip by Ambassador Claudio Fischer, the head of International Affairs and Multilingualism at the Parliamentary Services.
