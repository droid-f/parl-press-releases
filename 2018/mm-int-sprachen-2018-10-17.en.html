President of the National Council to attend international conference on direct democracy in Rome

Organised by the Swiss Institute in Rome in conjunction with the Swiss Embassy in Italy, the international conference entitled ‘Past, Present and Future of Direct Democracy. History, Models and Challenges for Direct Democracy in the Digital Era’ takes place in Rome on 19 and 20 October.

Content

​The president of the National Council, Dominique de Buman, will attend the international conference entitled ‘Past, Present and Future of Direct Democracy. History, Models and Challenges for Direct Democracy in the Digital Era’ taking place in Rome later this month (link).

Many well-known names from academia and politics, not only from Switzerland and Italy, but from all over the world, will come together at the Swiss Institute in Rome to discuss direct democracy and the challenges it faces at a time of digital revolution and new media.

Founded in 1947 with the support of the Confederation, the Swiss Institute in Rome fosters cultural and scientific exchange between Switzerland and Italy and is able to count on the support of a large number of institutional partners, including the State Secretariat for Education, Research and Innovation, the Swiss Arts Council Pro Helvetia, and the Federal Office of Culture.
