Press release

National Council president to pay official visit to Vienna

Content

​The president of the National Council, Dominique de Buman (CVP, FR), will pay an official visit to Vienna on 6 and 7 September. He will be received by Wolfgang Sobotka, the president of the Austrian National Council. This is a return visit after the two National Council presidents met in Bern in June. Meetings are set to take place with members of the Foreign Policy Committee, the Standing Subcommittee on EU Affairs and the Austria-Swiss Parliamentary Group. The two sides will exchange views on European policy issues, and bilateral relations between Austria and Switzerland. Mr de Buman will be accompanied by the president and vice president of the Delegation for Relations with the Austrian Parliament: National Council member Walter Müller (FDP, SG) and Council of States member Alex Kuprecht (SVP, SZ).

The close relations between Austria and Switzerland can be seen in the many numerous visits that take place at all levels. Over 40,000 Austrian nationals live in Switzerland, and a further 8,000 cross the border every day to work. There are currently around 16,000 Swiss nationals living in Austria. The two countries are mutually important trading partners. Furthermore, relations between the two parliaments were institutionalised in 2005 with the creation of two standing delegations. The Austria-Swiss Parliamentary Group and the Delegation for Relations with the Austrian Parliament foster relations with neighbouring state and meet on a regular basis. Members of both delegations will accompany Mr de Buman’s official visit to Vienna.
