EFTA/EU Delegation to attend COSAC Chairpersons’ Meeting

At the invitation of the Bulgarian presidency of the Council of the EU, a meeting will take place in Sofia on 21 and 22 January between the chairs of parliamentary com-mittees for union affairs of parliaments of the European Union, candidate countries and third countries, in particular EFTA/EEA countries. The Swiss EFTA/EU Delega-tion will be represented at the meeting by National Council member Kathy Riklin (CVP, ZH).

Content

The meeting will focus on the priorities set out by Bulgaria for its first presidency of the Council of the EU, which will be presented by the minister responsible, Ms Lilyana Pavlova. The priorities include efforts to bring about lasting economic growth and stronger social cohesion in the EU, stability and security in Europe, to provide Western Balkan countries with a European perspective and connectivity and to address issues relating to the digital economy.

The chairs of the committees for union affairs will also meet with Frans Timmermans, first vice president of the European Commission, and Bulgaria’s vice president, Ilijana Jotowa, to discuss the future of the European Union. The agenda also includes a discussion on the importance of European macro-regional strategies for sustainable development, stability and security.

The Conference of Parliamentary Committees for Union Affairs of Parliaments of the European Union (COSAC) was established in 1989 and since its formal recognition in the Treaty of Amsterdam in 1997 serves as a platform to intensify inter-parliamentary dialogue and represent members’ interests. Switzerland has been represented at the biannual meetings of the committees for union affairs since 2015.
