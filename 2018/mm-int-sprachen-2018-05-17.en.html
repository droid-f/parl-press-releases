Official visit by Karin Keller-Sutter, President of the Council of States, to the USA

The President of the Council of States, Karin Keller-Sutter (FDP, SG), will be paying an official visit to the USA between 21 and 24 May, which will take her to Washington D.C. (21 and 22 May), Chicago (23 May) and New Glarus (24 May). On the agenda are talks with representatives from the fields of politics, business and culture, as well as visits to a number of Swiss firms. Ms Keller-Sutter will be accompanied on the trip by a parliamentary delegation.

Content

At the beginning of the week, talks are set to take place with members of Congress, as well as with representatives from leading think tanks in the capital. Discussions will focus on Swiss-US trade relations and their prospects for the future, while addressing core principles of US financial market regulation and US tax reform measures and their impact on Switzerland as a location for international firms.

On 23 May Council of States president Karin Keller-Sutter will meet the Lieutenant Governor of Illinois and the Mayor of Chicago in the ‘windy city’ and hold talks with representatives from Swiss firms in the services, manufacturing and agricultural sector. Visits are also planned to the US headquarters of Zurich Insurance Group, the Swiss financial services company, Phonak and Emmi Roth, the US production facility of Emmi Holding AG.

On 24 May, the last day of the official visit, Ms Karin Keller-Sutter will travel to New Glarus, Wisconsin, where she will meet members of the Swiss community abroad to find out how they uphold their national and regional cultural identity. The village was founded in 1845 by 150 immigrants from its Swiss namesake. Many Swiss emigrants left for the USA in the nineteenth century.

The USA is a strategic economic and political partner for Switzerland. In 2017 Swiss businesses exported goods to the value of CHF 36.3 billion (12.3% of total exports) to the USA and imported goods valued at CHF 21.1 billion (8.0% of total imports). As such, the USA is Switzerland’s second largest trade partner. It is also the leading destination for Swiss direct investment abroad. At the end of 2016, Switzerland’s cumulative direct investment in the USA amounted to CHF 234 billion, 19.3 per cent of all Swiss direct investment abroad. Thanks to this investment, Swiss businesses have directly created around 500,000 jobs in the USA and a further 1.8 million jobs indirectly.

Ms Keller-Sutter will be accompanied on her visit by the second vice president of the Council of States, Géraldine Savary (SP, VD), Council of States members Thomas Hefti (FDP, GL), Robert Cramer (GPS, GE) and Anne Seydoux-Christe (CVP, JU), and National Council member Thomas Aeschi (SVP, ZG). The delegation will also be joined by the Dep. Secretary General of the Federal Assembly, Martina Buol, and the Dep. Head of International Affairs & Multilingualism, Cédric Stucky.
