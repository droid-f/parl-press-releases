Bringing the World to Graubünden

President of the National Council Martin Candinas (The Centre, GR) has invited the foreign ambassadors to Switzerland on a journey of discovery of his native Graubünden on Wednesday, 30 August.

Content

“It is important to me to communicate Switzerland's diversity to the outside world and to strengthen international relations at parliamentary level,” said President of the National Council Martin Candinas. That is why he is inviting the foreign ambassadors to the canton he comes from. On Wednesday, 30 August, the diplomats accredited to Switzerland will visit parts of Graubünden and discover the many sides of the mountainous canton. The programme will take the visitors from the globally active medtech company Hamilton to the picturesque Heidi village and finally to the official reception of the government of the Canton of Graubünden at the agricultural training centre Plantahof. The informal setting will provide an opportunity to promote dialogue among participants and to network.

note to the MEDIa

Journalists may attend the official event. Transportation will be organised by the Parliamentary Services. The number of participants is limited.
