Interparliamentary meeting between members of the Swiss and Austrian parliaments

The Delegation for Relations with the Austrian Parliament (Del-A), chaired by Alex Kuprecht (SVP, SZ), will receive its counterpart, the Austria-Switzerland Parliamentary Group, in Bern on 15 and 16 May 2023. The focus of this working visit will be an exchange of views on bilateral relations and on economic, security and energy policy issues.

Content

Switzerland and Austria maintain diverse and close relations and are closely linked through a common language and a lively economic and cultural exchange. The working meeting in Bern on 15 and 16 May 2023 will be an opportunity to cultivate these relations at parliamentary level. In addition to the talks between the two delegations, a meeting with the President of the Council of States, Brigitte Häberli-Koller, is planned.

In addition to the meeting at parliamentary level, a courtesy visit to Federal Councillor Albert Rösti, Head of the Federal Department of the Environment, Transport, Energy and Communications (DETEC), is planned to discuss energy policy issues and cooperation in the field of transport infrastructure. In view of the current security and defence policy challenges in Europe, there will also be a discussion with Joachim Adler, Head of Defence Policy at the Federal Department of Defence, Civil Protection and Sport (DDPS). Finally, the delegations will discuss economic developments with representatives of Economiesuisse.

The delegation from the Austria-Switzerland Parliamentary Group comprises its chairman Norbert Sieber (ÖVP) and the MPs Reinhold Einwallner (SPÖ), Gerald Loacker (NEOS), Karl Schmiedhofer (ÖVP), Jakob Schwarz (Grüne) and Thomas Spalt (FPÖ).
