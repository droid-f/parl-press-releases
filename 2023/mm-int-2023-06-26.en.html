Monday, 26 June 2023

Visits made abroad

President of the National Council Martin Candinas pays an official visit to Singapore and Vietnam

President of the National Council Martin Candinas (The Centre, GR) paid an official visit to Singapore from 26 to 27 June, and then to Vietnam from 28 to 30 June. He was accompanied by the first vice president, Eric Nussbaumer (SP, BL), the second vice president, Maja Riniker (FDP.The Liberals, AG), and National Council member Ida Glanzmann-Hunkeler (The Centre, LU). The focus of the various meetings was on foreign economic relations between Switzerland and these two member countries of the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN).

Content


In Singapore, Mr Candinas met with Deputy Speaker of Parliament Jessica Tan and Minister for Trade and Industry Gan Kim Yong. Discussions focused on the digital trade agreement currently being negotiated between Singapore and the countries of the European Free Trade Association (EFTA), of which Switzerland is a member; development of trade relations between Switzerland and Singapore; cooperation in innovation and education; security tensions in the region and the effects of climate change.

The Swiss delegation also met the management of Singapore transport company SMRT, one of the leading operators in the city-state, and visited the campus of the Ecole hôtelière de Lausanne (EHL) and the ETH-Centre in Singapore. Extensive discussions with staff from these two institutions gave the members of the delegation an insight into the opportunities that exist in Southeast Asia for tourism and research, as well as the importance for Switzerland of having centres of expertise in Singapore. Mr Candinas and his delegation were also able to meet representatives of the Swiss community, Swiss companies and institutions in Singapore at the Swiss Club.

On the second part of his official visit to Southeast Asia, Mr Candinas travelled to Hanoi in Vietnam. There he was received by President of the Vietnamese National Assembly Vương Đình Huệ, President of Vietnam Võ Văn Thưởng, Prime Minister Pham Minh Chinh and Minister of Industry and Trade Nguyễn Hồng Diên.

During the talks with the high-ranking officials, Mr Candinas expressed Switzerland’s willingness to rapidly advance the negotiations on a progressive and ambitious free trade agreement between Vietnam and the EFTA countries. The Vietnamese authorities also called for the two countries to work more closely together in areas such as energy transition, sustainable development, digitalisation and higher education.

As part of its 2021-24 economic development cooperation, Switzerland is investing some CHF 70 million in Vietnam. The delegation visited projects supported by the State Secretariat for Economic Affairs (SECO), including a distribution centre (Minh Thái Ltd Co) and a retailer (Yến Biên Mart), both benefiting from the Supply Chain Financing project, which aims to facilitate access to financing for these companies in the supply chain. The delegation also visited a large textile factory (Regent Garment Factory) which participates in the International Labour Organization's (ILO) Better Work programme. The programme helps the company to improve working conditions and social dialogue, and to provide ongoing training for male and female workers, with an emphasis on women's empowerment and gender equality. A visit to VinFast's state-of-the-art electric vehicle production facility in Hai Phong showed the delegation the strategic role of Swiss technology and innovation in Vietnam's economic boom.

Mr Candinas and his delegation also took part in various meetings organised by the Embassy of Switzerland with representatives of Swiss companies based in Vietnam, and were able to gain insight into the daily challenges faced by these companies, as well as the economic and commercial potential that Vietnam still has to offer them.​
