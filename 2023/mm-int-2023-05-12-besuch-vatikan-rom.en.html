Official visit to the Vatican and Rome by Federal Assembly presidents Martin Candinas and Brigitte Häberli-Koller

On Saturday 6 May, National Council president Martin Candinas (The Centre, GR) and Council of States president Brigitte Häberli-Koller (The Centre, TG) attended the swearing-in ceremony of the recruits of the Pontifical Swiss Guard at the Vatican. Mr Candinas also held talks in Rome, including with the president of the Chamber of Deputies, Lorenzo Fontana.

Content

The presidents of the National Council and the Council of States, along with Viola Amherd, Vice President of the Federal Council, and Thomas Süssli, Chief of the Swiss Armed Forces, attended the swearing-in ceremony of 23 recruits of the Pontifical Swiss Guard, held on 6 May each year in memory of the soldiers who died during the Sack of Rome in 1527. Mr Candinas and Ms Häberli-Koller were received by Pope Francis during their itinerary, which also included a visit to the Swiss Institute in Rome. Founded in 1947 with the support of the Confederation, the Swiss Institute fosters cultural and scientific dialogue between Switzerland and Italy. It receives support from many institutional partners, including the State Secretariat for Education, Research and Innovation and the Swiss Arts Council Pro Helvetia.

On Monday 8 May, on the fringes of the celebrations at the Vatican, Mr Candinas was received in Rome by his Italian counterpart, Lorenzo Fontana, President of the Chamber of Deputies. This was the first high-level parliamentary meeting since Italy's general election held in September 2022. The talks focused on topics including current bilateral and international issues and relations with the European Union. In particular, Mr Candinas and Mr Fontana discussed the Chamber of Deputies’ recent adoption of the agreement on the taxation of cross-border commuters, which was signed in 2020. Mr Candinas expressed his satisfaction that the agreement had been approved, and also his hope that Senate approval would follow quickly. This would conclude the process and allow Switzerland to be removed from the 1999 Italian blacklist regarding the taxation of natural persons. It would also regulate remote working for cross-border commuters until 30 June 2023.

The meeting is a manifestation of the mutual desire to maintain regular contact at parliamentary level, following the official visit to Bern by the former president of the Italian Chamber of Deputies, Roberto Fico, and the meeting in Rome with the former president of the Italian senate, Maria Elisabetta Alberti Casellati, which both took place in 2022.

In Rome, Mr Candinas was accompanied by the chair and vice chair of the Delegation for Relations with the Italian Parliament (Del-I), Greta Gysin (National Council, The Greens, TI) and Andrea Caroni (Council of States, FDP, AR). During their visit the delegation was also received by Toni Ricciardi, member of the Chamber of Deputies and chairman of the recently re-constituted Italy-Switzerland Friendship Section, in the presence of members of the two chambers of the Italian parliament.

Mr Candinas also met with representatives of the Permanent Mission of Switzerland to the United Nations organisations and visited the headquarters of the UN Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) while in Rome. The FAO, of which Switzerland has been a member since 1946, is dedicated to improving food security and nutrition, rural livelihoods and the sustainable management and use of natural resources through international coordination, technical support and research networks.

Finally, the president of the National Council visited the Swiss school in Rome, which provides education up to baccalaureate level. Swiss schools abroad are supported by the Swiss Confederation (Federal Department of Home Affairs’ Office of Culture), and each receives educational and pedagogical advice from one of the cantons according to the federalist structure.
