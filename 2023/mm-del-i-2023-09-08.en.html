Working meeting of the Delegation of the Italian Parliament for relations with Switzerland

The Delegation for Relations with the Italian Parliament (Del-I) chaired by Greta Gysin (Green Party, TI) will receive its counterpart, the Italy-Switzerland Parliamentary Group, in Bern from 11 to 13 September. The focus of this working visit will be an exchange of views on bilateral relations and on current regional, national and international issues.

Content

Bilateral relations between Switzerland and Italy are characterised by close economic political, social and cultural ties, a common language and regular contacts at all levels. This working visit follows a working visit to Rome by the Del-I in November 2021. On the sidelines of the parliamentary-level exchange, a courtesy visit to National Council President Martin Candinas (The Centre, GR) and to Federal Councillor Ignazio Cassis, head of the Federal Department of Foreign Affairs (FDFA), is also planned.

The delegation's programme also includes a visit to Zurich, where they will participate in an exchange on the topic of bilateral cooperation in science and academia and visit the exhibition ‘Italianità. Experiences of Switzerland’ at the Swiss National Museum.

The Delegation of the Italian-Swiss bilateral Friendship Section is composed of its chairman, Toni Ricciardi (Partito Democratico) and MPs Enrico Borghi (Azione-ItaliaViva-RenewEurope), Giangiacomo Calovini (Fratelli d’Italia), Emiliano Fenu (Movimento 5 Stelle), Franco Manes (Misto-Minoranze linguistiche) and Catia Polidori (Forza Italia – Partito popolare europeo).

The Delegation for relations with the Italian Parliament is the counterpart of the Italian-Swiss bilateral Friendship Section of the Italian Parliament. The Friendship Section has 21 members from all political parties represented in the Italian Chamber of Deputies and the Senate of the Republic. Switzerland maintains close relations with its neighbouring countries. The delegations for relations with parliaments of other states foster bilateral interparliamentary dialogue, and parliamentary presidents regularly make official visits. This includes Italy: Switzerland’s southern neighbour is its third-largest trading partner, and Italians make up the largest group of foreign nationals living in Switzerland.
