The EFTA/EU delegation in Norway for the EFTA ministerial meeting

On Monday 23 and Tuesday 24 June, EFTA's Parliamentary Committee and the EFTA Consultative Committee will meet with trade ministers of the four EFTA member states in Tromsø, Norway. Participants will take the opportunity to discuss the state of ongoing free trade negotiations with third countries as well as international developments.

Content

The Parliamentary Committee, the Consultative Committee and the trade ministers of the member states of the European Free Trade Association EFTA (Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway and Switzerland) will meet in Tromsø for their annual meeting. Participants will have the opportunity to discuss the status of EFTA free trade agreements currently under negotiation with a number of countries and economic blocs, such as Mercosur, Vietnam and Singapore, as well as those recently concluded with Ukraine, Thailand and Malaysia. Negotiations with Malaysia are now complete and the new agreement has been signed when the two parties' trade ministers met on Monday morning. Other meetings being held within EFTA's advisory bodies will provide an opportunity to take stock of many other economic policy issues of particular interest to Switzerland, such as current geopolitical tensions and their impact on international trade, and more specifically trade relations between the United States and the EU and the UK. ​

What are the EFTA advisory bodies? The Parliamentary Committee is made up of parliamentarians from the four EFTA member states, while the Consultative Committee is made up of representatives of trade unions and employers' associations from these countries. The two committees are EFTA's advisory bodies and are important platforms for dialogue and consultation on the social, economic and political aspects of free trade agreements and EFTA's strategic guidelines.

The members of the delegation are Council of State members Damian Müller (FDP, LU), Vice-President of the Delegation, Hannes Germann (SVP, SH), and Carlo Sommaruga (SP, GE), as well as National Council member Hans-Peter Portmann (FDP, ZH). They are attending the meeting in Tromsø as representatives of the Swiss Parliament in the EFTA Parliamentary Committee.

The delegation's second northern mission

On 3 and 4 July, National Council member Hans-Peter Portmann (FDP, ZH) will travel to Copenhagen, Denmark, to attend the meeting of the Presidents of COSAC, the Conference of Parliamentary Committees for European Affairs, in which the Swiss delegation has observer status. Denmark will hold the Presidency of the Council of the European Union in the second half of 2025.​
