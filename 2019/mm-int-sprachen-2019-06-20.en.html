Official visit to Mongolia by President of the National Council

The president of the National Council, Marina Carobbio Guscetti (SP, TI), will visit Mongolia in an official capacity from 24 June to 3 July. She will engage in official talks while in the capital Ulaanbaatar before travelling to the north of the country to visit projects run as part of Switzerland’s development cooperation.

Content

​Ms Carobbio Guscetti’s trip to Mongolia is a reciprocal visit, Mr Gombojay Zandanshatar, Chairman of the Mongolian parliament, and an accompanying delegation having paid an official visit to Bern in March 2019.

On 24 and 25 June Ms Carobbio will attend talks in Ulaanbaatar with Mr Zandanshatar and other members of the Mongolian parliament, with President Khaltmaagiin Battulga, Prime Minister Uchnaagiin Khürelsüch and Foreign Minister Damdin Tsogtbaatar. On 25 June she will give a speech on Women in Politics at the Women Leadership Forum taking place in the Mongolian capital.

Both in Ulaanbaatar and in the provinces of Uvs and Khovd in the north of the country, Ms Carobbio Guscetti will visit several projects which receive funding from Switzerland, primarily from the Swiss Agency for Development and Cooperation. These include projects in the field of mining, health, clean air, agriculture, education and administration. Meetings and discussions with local authorities are also on the agenda.

Relations between Switzerland and Mongolia date back to the late 1950s, when the first Swiss businesses set up commercial relations with the Asian country. Switzerland was Mongolia’s first non-socialist commercial partner. It officially recognised the Democratic Republic of Mongolia on 20 May 1964. Relations have become closer since Mongolia embraced democracy in the early 1990s. When Mongolia suffered some particularly harsh winters at the end of that decade, Switzerland provided humanitarian aid, and since 2002 this support has taken the form of development cooperation.
