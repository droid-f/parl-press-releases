Friday, 22 March 2019 15h00

Press release

Official visit to Spain and Portugal by a parliamentary delegation led by the president of the Council of States, Jean-René Fournier

From 25 to 26 March, the president of the Council of States, Jean-René Fournier (CVP/VS), will be in Spain at the invitation of his counterpart Pío García-Escudero Márquez. On 27 March, he will continue to Lisbon for a two-day visit to mark the hundredth anniversary of diplomatic relations between Switzerland and Portugal. Mr Fournier will be accompanied by a Council of States delegation. Discussions will focus primarily on bilateral relations and on a range of regional and global issues.

Content

​On 25 March Jean-René Fournier and a Council of States delegation will be in Madrid for discussions with the president of the Spanish senate, Pío García-Escudero Márquez, and with EU Secretary of State Luis Marco Aguiriano. During their stay in the Spanish capital the delegation will visit the Congress of Deputies, where they will be welcomed by its vice president, José Ignacio Prendes Prendes. The main purpose of these various official meetings is to acknowledge the excellent relationship between Spain and Switzerland, in particular economic and institutional relations. The delegation will also meet Manuela Carmena, mayor of the Spanish capital, to discuss Madrid Central, a pioneering project for Spain intended to restrict traffic and so reduce pollution in the centre of the capital.

On 26 March the delegation will continue to Toleda, a historical city on the UNESCO World Heritage List, where they will meet the city’s mayor, Milagros Tolón. Toleda is also the capital of the province of the same name and of the autonomous community of Castilla-La Mancha.

This year Switzerland and Portugal are celebrating a century of fruitful bilateral diplomatic relations. Jean-René Fournier is going to Lisbon on 27 and 28 March at the invitation of Eduardo Ferro Rodrigues, President of the Assembly of the Republic. Mr Fournier and his delegation will take part in a welcome ceremony in the Portuguese parliament, which will be followed by talks with the chair of the Committee on Foreign Affairs and Portuguese Communities, Sérgio Sousa Pinto, and the chair of the European Affairs Committee, Regina Bastos. On the following day the delegation will meet Ana Paula Zacarias, State Secretary for European Affairs, and José Luís Carneiro,Secretary of State ofPortuguese Communities Abroad. The delegation will also take the opportunity provided by this official working visit to meet members of the Swiss community and members of the Portugal-Switzerland Parliamentary Friendship Group. A courtesy visit to President of the Republic Marcelo Rebelo de Sousa will round off the programme of the official visit.

The members of the Swiss Council of States delegation will be as follows:

  • Jean-René Fournier, president, CVP
  • Hans Stöckli, first vice president, SP
  • Thomas Hefti, FDP
  • Brigitte Häberli-Koller, CVP
  • Roland Eberle, SVP
  • Robert Cramer, Green Party
  • Werner Luginbühl, BDP
