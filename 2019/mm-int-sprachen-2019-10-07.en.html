Official visit by President of the Council of States to Azerbaijan and Russia

The president of the Council of States, Jean-René Fournier (CVP/VS), will pay an official visit to Azerbaijan from 8 to 11 October, and to Russia from 12 to 19 October. He will be received by the president of the Azerbaijan parliament, and by the president of Azerbaijan and his minister of foreign affairs, as well as by his Russian counterpart, Valentina Matviyenko and the president of the Duma, Vyatcheslav Volodin.

Content

​At official talks in Baku, Mr Fournier will discuss Azerbaijan's strategic geographical position as the ‘central corridor’ of the New Silk Road between China and Europe. Azerbaijan is currently investing heavily in the development of its infrastructure, with the construction of the new port in Baku and the opening of the Baku-Tbilisi (Georgia) – Kars (Turkey) railway line. Switzerland and Azerbaijan maintain regular bilateral contacts and collaborate actively within the Bretton Woods institutions, where they are members of the same voting group. They are also partners in technical cooperation projects.

During his stay, Mr Fournier will visit a centre funded by the Swiss Agency for Development and Cooperation (SDC) that helps socially disadvantaged women to enter the labour market. The programme also includes a visit to the production sites of the Swiss companies HOLCIM and SIKA in Azerbaijan.

In Moscow, Mr Fournier will be received by Valentina Matviyenko, chair of the Federation Council, the Russian parliament’s upper house, and by Vyatcheslav Volodin, president of the Duma, the lower house. Talks will focus on the state of bilateral relations, international political issues (the conflict in Ukraine, Syria, Iran and Venezuela), the security situation in Europe and Russia's return to the Parliamentary Assembly of the Council of Europe. Mr Fournier will also meet Patriarch Kirill of the Russian Orthodox Church and Alexander Beglov, the governor of St Petersburg.

In 2013, the Council of States and the Russian Federation Council signed a memorandum of intent to expand collaboration between the two parliamentary chambers. Mr Fournier is visiting Russia at the invitation of Ms Matviyenko, following on from her working visit to Bern on 28 June.
