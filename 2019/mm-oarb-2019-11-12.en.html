Press release

Open day at the parliament building

Free admission to the parliament building in Bern, Saturday 16 November, 9am to 4pm

Content

​Building on the success of the previous open day events, Parliamentary Services is happy to renew the invitation to an open day event at the parliament building on Saturday 16 November from 9am to 4pm. You do not need to book a ticket in advance for this event. Visitors will be free to wander through parliament building without a guided tour.

Visitors on that day will be the first to have the opportunity to visit the exhibition in the parliament building: ‘Eine Schweiz für Kinder. Wirklich?’ (part of a campaign to promote child upbringing free from violence) by the Swiss child protection foundation ‘Kinderschutz Schweiz’.

Tour the exhibition with a member of Kinderschutz Schweiz as a guide (the tour lasts about 60 minutes, participants should gather at the visitors’ entrance).

Visits in German are at: 10am / 12am / 2pm / 4pm; and in French at: 11am / 1pm / 3pm. Tours are limited to a maximum of 30 visitors. Registration is on site.

As at every open day event, a member of our team will be on hand in each room to provide information about the history and the architecture of the parliament building, about the Swiss political system and about the building’s two most famous paintings: one in the chamber of the Council of States depicting a people’s assembly, and the other in the chamber of the National Council depicting the ‘cradle of the Confederation’.

You might also stop by at the elegant cafeteria in the former reading room during your visit for refreshments or a meal.
