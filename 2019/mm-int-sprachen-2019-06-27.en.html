President of Russia’s Federation Council on a working visit to Bern

On 28 June, the president of the Council of States, Jean-René Fournier (CVP/VS), will welcome the president of Russia’s Federation Council, Valentina Matvienko, and an accompanying parliamentary delegation to Bern. This working visit will take place on the fringes of the 130th anniversary celebrations of the Inter-Parliamentary Union in Geneva, which will be attended by Ms Matvienko.

Content

​Jean-René Fournier, the president of the Council of States, will hold talks with Valentina Matvienko, the president of the Federation Council (upper chamber of the Russian parliament) on Friday 28 June in the Bellevue Palace Hotel in Bern. Both Swiss and Russian parliamentary delegations will also be present. Bilateral relations between Switzerland and Russia, international issues and the current geopolitical situation will be on the agenda. Other Swiss politicians taking part in the talks include Mr Filippo Lombardi (CVP/TI), member of the Council of States and chair of the Foreign Affairs Committee; Margret Kiener-Nellen (SP/BE), member of the National Council and head of the Swiss Delegation to the Parliamentary Assembly of the OSCE; and Fabian Molina (SP/ZH), member of the National Council and co-chair of the Swiss-Russian inter-parliamentary friendship group.

From 6pm to 6.30pm the press will have the opportunity to take photos and conduct individual interviews in the lobby of the Bellevue Palace Hotel.

Representatives of the two countries regularly meet and communicate both at a parliamentary and governmental level. In mid-October, Ms Matvienko will receive her counterpart Mr Fournier in Moscow as he travels in an official capacity in Russia and Azerbaijan.
