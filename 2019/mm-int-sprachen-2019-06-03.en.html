Press release

President of the Assembly of North Macedonia received in Bern

The president of the National Council, Marina Carobbio Guscetti (SP/TI), will receive the president of the Assembly of North Macedonia and his delegation in Bern on 5 June. North Macedonia is a priority country in terms of Swiss cooperation with strong contacts and ties to Switzerland, particularly due to its large diaspora. Some 66,000 people with North Macedonian origins live in Switzerland.

Content

​On 5 June, the president of the National Council will meet the president of the Assembly of the Republic of North Macedonia, Talat Xhaferi, who is from the Albanian minority, which makes up 25% of the country’s population.

Talks between Marina Carobbio and Talat Xhaferi will focus on the close relations between the two countries and development cooperation. They will also discuss the country’s prospects of joining the EU and NATO, the treatment of minorities on both sides, and the situation in the western Balkans.

During his official visit to Switzerland, Mr Xhaferi will also have the opportunity to meet the president of the Council of States, Jean-René Fournier (CVP/VS), the deputy state secretary of the Federal Department of Foreign Affairs (FDFA), Krystyna Marty Lang, and the delegate for multilingualism, Nicoletta Mariolini.

On 17 June 2018, North Macedonia signed an historic agreement with Greece on its change of name, paving the way for accession to the EU and NATO.
