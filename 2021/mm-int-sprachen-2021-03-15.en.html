President of the Slovenian parliament to visit Bern

The president of the National Council, Andreas Aebi (BE/SVP), will receive the president of the Slovenian parliament, Igor Zorčič, on an official visit on 17 March. This meeting provides an opportunity for direct discussions with Slovenia, which will assume the presidency of the Council of the European Union in the second half of 2021. The talks will address the current health situation, the role of the parliaments during the COVID-19 crisis and Slovenia's priorities for its presidency of the EU Council.

Content

​The president of the Slovenian Parliament, Igor Zorčič, will pay a reciprocal visit to Bern following that of the former president of the National Council, Marina Carobbio Guscetti (SP/TI), to Ljubljana in May 2019. The official meeting on 17 March between National Council president Andreas Aebi and his Slovenian counterpart will provide an opportunity to discuss the role and functioning of parliaments during the pandemic, and also to learn more about Slovenia's priorities when it assumes the presidency of the EU Council. Of particular interest are the Next Generation EU recovery plan and the process of EU enlargement to include the countries of the Western Balkans. The two parliamentary presidents will also discuss relations between Switzerland and the European Union and the situation regarding the institutional framework agreement.

After his official meeting with Mr Aebi, Mr Zorčič will pay courtesy visits to Guy Parmelin, President of the Swiss Confederation, and Alex Kuprecht (SVP/SZ), President of the Council of States.

lovenia is an important partner for Switzerland: there is excellent cooperation between the two countries, both bilaterally and multilaterally. As part of its first contribution to EU enlargement, Switzerland been involved in various projects in Slovenia in the areas of renewable energies and health. Trade relations between the two countries have also rebounded since 2018 thanks to exports of Swiss chemicals and pharmaceuticals to Slovenia.
