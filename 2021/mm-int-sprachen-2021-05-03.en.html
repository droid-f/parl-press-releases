President of the Austrian National Council to pay visit to Bern

On 5 May, National Council president Andreas Aebi will receive his Austrian counterpart, Wolfgang Sobotka, in Bern. The focus of discussions during the visit will be on bilateral relations and an exchange of experiences on efforts to fight the COVID-19 pandemic.

Content

​The president of the Austrian National Council, Wolfgang Sobotka, will be received by National Council president Andreas Aebi on the fringes of the special session of Parliament in Bern. The close and friendly relations between Austria and Switzerland are characterised by frequent contacts at all levels. In light of the COVID-19 pandemic, exchanges between the two neighbouring countries at parliamentary level have further intensified.

Among other things, the visit will focus on an exchange of experiences on the measures taken by both countries to fight the COVID-19 pandemic. The two National Council presidents exchanged views last December via video conference on the functioning of parliaments during the pandemic.

Mr Sobotka will be accompanied on his visit to Switzerland by the chair of the Austrian-Swiss Parliamentary Group, National Council member Norbert Sieber. Also on the agenda is a meeting with Council of States president Alex Kuprecht, who chairs the Delegation for Relations with the Austrian Parliament (Del-A). National Council member Nicolo Paganini, vice president of the Delegation for Relations with the Austrian Parliament, will also take part in the discussions. To round off the visit, the Austrian delegation will then pay a courtesy visit to Federal Councillor Alain Berset, the head of the Federal Department of Home Affairs.
