Press release

Andreas Aebi welcomes the president of the Kosovo parliament

The president of the National Council, Andreas Aebi (SVP, BE), will receive Vjosa Osmani, president of the Kosovo parliament, in Bern on 3 March 2021. This will be Ms Osmani‘s first official visit abroad since the election victory two weeks ago.

Content

Vjosa Osmani’s official visit to Bern is a special one: the invitation for a reciprocal parliamentary visit was extended to the president of the Kosovo parliament, but since November 2020 Ms Osmani has also been Acting President of the Republic of Kosovo.

During the official reception in Bern, Mr Aebi and Ms Osmani will discuss the election held on 14 February and Switzerland’s cooperation with Kosovo. The talks will further focus on measures for fighting the COVID-19 pandemic and the normalisation of relations between Kosovo and Serbia. Also on the programme is a courtesy visit to the president of the Council of States, Alex Kuprecht (SVP, SZ), and to Federal Councilor Ignazio Cassis, head of Federal Department of Foreign Affairs.

Switzerland recognised Kosovo as an independent republic exactly 13 years ago, on 27 February 2008. The young state is one of the priority countries of the Swiss Agency for Development and Cooperation (SDC) under Swiss cooperation with Eastern Europe. The Swiss Armed Forces have also been present in the country since 1999 as part of a peacekeeping mission there. In June 2020 the Swiss parliament voted to extend the Swisscoy mission, comprising 195 military personnel, until 2023. Switzerland and Kosovo already enjoy close ties on several levels, partly owing to the large Kosovar community in Switzerland. The official visit testifies to the determination of both countries to strengthen this bilateral relationship, including at the parliamentary level.

Information for journalists: Following the working visit, Mr Aebi and Ms Osmai will be available to answer questions. The point de presse will take place on 3 March 2021 at 10:40am in the Salon Royal of the Bellevue Hotel. If you would like to participate, please inform the Parliamentary Services beforehand (information@parl.admin.ch).
